## [1.0.0] - 2024-12-01

### Added

#### 转录技术 (Transcription)
- `whisper-transcription.md` - Local video transcription with OpenAI Whisper
- `youtube-transcript.md` - YouTube video transcript extraction
- `meeting-transcription.md` - Meeting and interview transcription with speaker diarization

#### 内容提取 (Content Extraction)
- `video-summarization.md` - Video content summarization techniques
- `key-points-extraction.md` - Key points and insights extraction
- `chapter-generation.md` - Video chapter and timestamp generation

#### 笔记整理 (Note Organization)
- `video-notes.md` - Structured note-taking from video content
- `knowledge-extraction.md` - Knowledge extraction and organization

#### 字幕处理 (Subtitle Processing)
- `subtitle-generation.md` - Automatic subtitle generation in multiple formats
- `subtitle-translation.md` - Subtitle translation and bilingual subtitle creation

#### 学习辅助 (Learning Aid)
- `lecture-notes.md` - Lecture and course note-taking with active recall
- `tutorial-documentation.md` - Tutorial documentation generation from videos

#### Community Files
- README.md with bilingual documentation
- CI/CD pipeline with GitHub Actions
- Issue templates and PR templates
- Contributing guidelines
